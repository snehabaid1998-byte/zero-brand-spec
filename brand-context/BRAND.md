# Zero brand context (spec v0.1)

Spec work by Sneha Baid. Not affiliated with Zero. Values come from zero.inc's own CSS, read on 20 Nov 2026.

## The idea in one breath

Old CRMs are prehistoric. Zero makes CRM history. Everything plays with that gap: stone-age motifs next to a product that runs itself.

## Voice

- Plain and specific. "Log it into seventeen fields", not "streamline your workflow".
- Honest about trade-offs. Zero's compare pages say where the competitor wins. Keep doing that.
- Funny through the metaphor, never through exclamation marks. One joke per piece is plenty.
- Numbers only when a published customer story backs them. Quote them as the story states them.
- Do not call Zero an AI SDR, a point solution or only an enrichment tool. It is a complete CRM with agents working on it.

## Look

- Light canvas `#ece9ea`, brand ink `#2e0000`. Dark sections use void `#010101` with sand `#c9b8a0`.
- Headings in Geomanist 700, body in Geist, prompts and data in Geist Mono.
- Show the real product working wherever you can. A live UI beats a screenshot, and a screenshot beats an illustration.
- Motifs: cave-line figures, halftone dot clusters, pixel sprites (the mammoth), one chrome orb. Use one or two per piece, not all four.

## Never

- Purple gradients, glassmorphism stacks, stock 3D blobs. That is the 2019 SaaS template.
- More than one accent colour on a page.
- Claims about customers that are not in a published story.

## Markets

Pick the market before writing. See `tokens.json > markets` for date formats, CTAs and the trust line. For EU audiences, lead with data staying in the EU by default.

## Check before you ship

1. Only token colours used? Run the colour lint.
2. Product shown, not described?
3. Would it still make sense printed three metres wide on a wall?
